Business-focused engineering
When a customer chooses ETEK instead of the alternative, they are choosing business-focused engineering and technology solutions designed around their actual requirements.

Integrated systems

How we work

A delivery process you can follow from start to live

  1. Requirements

    Understand

    We start with your requirements, your current processes and the problem you actually need solved.

  2. Scope

    Plan

    Scope, priorities and how the solution fits alongside the systems you already run.

  3. Blueprint

    Design

    System and interface design, so the solution is clear to the people who will use it every day.

  4. Build

    Develop

    Engineering the solution, with your business processes driving how it is built.

  5. Verification

    Test

    Checking the system against the requirements before it goes anywhere near live operations.

  6. Go live

    Deploy

    Putting the solution into use, ready to keep working as the business changes.
